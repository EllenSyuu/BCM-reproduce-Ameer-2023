1. Model objective and observation types

The latent quantity is the out-of-school rate for country c, relative age g, and entry/cohort year e. The model combines administrative observations and household/census survey observations while allowing administrative error, regional error, survey bias and survey non-sampling error.

2. Cohort structure: latent mean and cohort change

The entry-age baseline process is represented by a spline over entry/cohort year. The current implementation uses the basis matrices B1 and B5 constructed by spline_basis.py.

Mc,e = Σk=1NK1 μ*c,kB1e,k
μ*c,k ~ N(γc + μ*c,k-1, σμ2),   k > 1
γc ~ N-(0, σγ,r(c)2)Country drift is constrained to be non-positive; r(c) is the UIS SDG region assigned to country c.
μ*c,k(2000) ~ N(0, 22)The implementation anchors the annual spline coefficient whose Greville abscissa is 2000 and constructs the random walk outward in both directions. This coefficient convention still needs confirmation against UIS source code; it is not identical to placing the prior on the evaluated spline value at year 2000.

3. Age-by-cohort change: two-dimensional P-spline

For ages after the entry age, the change from the baseline is represented by a cumulative age-by-entry-year surface. The free coefficient surface is αc,g,k.

Θc,g,e = Σk=1NK5 B5e,k Σh=1g αc,h,k
κc,0,e = Mc,e;    κc,g,e = Mc,e + Θc,g,e,   g > 0
αc,g,k - αc,g-1,k ~ N(0, τα1-1)
αc,g,k - αc,g,k-1 ~ N(0, τα2-1)
Σgαc,g,k ~ N(0, 22)These three equations are implemented as NumPyro factor penalties corresponding to the Stan target increments.

4. Administrative likelihood (Admin-only and Full models)

For an administrative observation i, the model is on the original rate scale. The regional error term is shared by observations from the same UIS SDG region.

Ai ~ N(Φ(κc(i),g(i),e(i)), σadm2 + ωr(i)2)
Ai = 1 - Ec,y,a,s / Pc,y,a,sE is total enrolment summed across the applicable education levels; P is the matching single-age population denominator.
σadm ~ HalfNormal(σσ);   ωr ~ HalfNormal(σσ)

9. Readiness and remaining work

  1. MF, M and F input arrays have been assembled for 213 countries using administrative, DHS, MICS and Census observations and 7 UIS SDG regions. WIDE/“Other” is excluded because available aggregate rows do not provide single-age observations or clustered-Jackknife uncertainty; any fit is therefore partial.
  2. The year-2000 prior is assigned to the annual spline control coefficient whose Greville abscissa is 2000. Verify this coefficient interpretation and the cubic spline/boundary convention against UIS implementation details if available.
  3. The revised non-centered model passed a four-chain, 14-country MF pilot spanning all seven regions (1,000 warmup and 1,000 retained draws per chain, no thinning, target acceptance 0.99): max R-hat 1.0073, min n_eff 885.4, zero divergences, zero invalid diagnostics.
  4. A controlled rerun of the original eight-country subset still failed (max R-hat 1.0187, min n_eff 268.8, zero divergences). Its worst term was the MICS2 error scale eta_w[5]; this subset has only 24 MICS2 observations across COD and TCD, and COD has one administrative observation, indicating weak support for that subset.
  5. Run corresponding M and F subset pilots and review diagnostics before any full-country fits. Continue to label outputs partial until WIDE/“Other” is represented with a compatible likelihood. Compare posterior output with ROFST.MOD only after the model and source-vintage assumptions are confirmed.
