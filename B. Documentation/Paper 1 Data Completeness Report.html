1. Executive conclusion

Inputs have been assembled for a preliminary fit: the current MF input has 213 countries, 7 UIS SDG regions, 37,060 administrative observations and 6,028 DHS/MICS/Census observations. Male and female inputs have also been built. The input builder applies the selected region, age, sex and observation filters.
This is not yet a complete paper reproduction: WIDE/“Other” is excluded because available aggregates do not provide the model's single-age observations and clustered-Jackknife standard errors. Colombia ECV and Papua New Guinea HIES also remain unavailable. WIDE rows are retained for future work but are not included in Eq. 2.

7. Remaining factors before a full reproduction fit

  1. Verify the age-2000 prior interpretation and annual spline degree/boundary convention against paper details or UIS code.
  2. Decide whether compatible single-age WIDE data and clustered-Jackknife uncertainty can be obtained; otherwise report results as a partial reproduction.
  3. The revised four-chain, 14-country MF pilot spans all seven SDG regions and passed: 1,000 warmup and 1,000 retained draws per chain, no thinning, target acceptance 0.99, max R-hat 1.0073, min n_eff 885.4, zero divergences and no invalid diagnostics.
  4. A controlled rerun of the original eight-country subset still failed (max R-hat 1.0187, min n_eff 268.8, zero divergences). The limiting term is the MICS2 wave-error scale; the subset has only 24 MICS2 cells across COD and TCD, and COD has only one administrative observation, indicating weak support in that small subset.
  5. Run corresponding M and F subset pilots and review diagnostics before full-country fits. The passing MF pilot is not evidence that all sex-specific full fits will converge.
Current status: a regionally stratified MF pilot passes convergence checks, while the smaller original subset remains weakly identified. Exact spline/anchor conventions still need confirmation, and WIDE/“Other” is not in the likelihood. No full-country fits have been run; current outputs remain partial-reproduction pilot results.
